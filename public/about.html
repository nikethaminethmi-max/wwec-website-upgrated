Our story and vision

World Wide Engineering & Constructions (Pvt) Ltd has delivered sustainable, innovative construction in Sri Lanka for more than 26 years.

We began as a small builder and grew into a one-stop service provider, taking each project from first design to final handover.

Today our teams combine modern methods, prefabricated systems, and careful site supervision to build homes and developments that last.

Deliver quality, cost-effective construction with honest service and professional standards.

Our vision
